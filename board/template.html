    What verify means

    open means the posting page returned 200 without a closed phrase at build time. unchecked means there was no page to check (a Hacker News comment, a Reddit post).

    Postings are deduped by URL and by company plus title, with sources unioned. Nothing here is invented; every row keeps its source URL.

    Your marks stay on this machine

    Applied, skipped and starred marks live in this browser's local storage under one key. Rebuilding the page keeps them. Clearing site data loses them, so export applied as text into the vault when you finish a session.
